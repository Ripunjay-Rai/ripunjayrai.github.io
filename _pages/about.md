<h1 style="font-size: 36px; color: #0F766E;"> About Me </h1>



I am Ripunjay Rai, a physicist specializing in computational materials science, first-principles simulations, and atomistic modeling. I completed my M.Sc. in Physics at the **Central University of Punjab**, with a CGPA of **7.95/10**.

I am currently working as a Project Associate at the **Centre for Nano and Material Sciences (CNMS), Jain (Deemed-to-be University), Bengaluru, India**. My current research focuses on *the development of machine-learning interatomic potentials for metal clusters*, with an emphasis on combining first-principles calculations and machine-learning approaches for efficient and reliable atomistic simulations.

My broader research interests include density functional theory (DFT), electronic structure theory, computational materials science, and machine-learning methods for atomistic modeling.

<h1 style="font-size: 36px; color: #0F766E;"> Current Research </h1>

My current research focuses on machine-learning interatomic potentials for metal clusters. These potentials aim to provide computationally efficient representations of atomic interactions, enabling atomistic simulations of systems that may be computationally demanding for direct first-principles methods. I am interested in developing reliable computational models informed by density functional theory (DFT) and evaluating their ability to describe the structural and energetic properties of atomistic systems.
